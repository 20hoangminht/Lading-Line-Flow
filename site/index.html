How it works

1

Documents arrive the way they already do

Email, file drop or SFTP. Commercial invoices, packing lists, house and master bills of lading, arrival notices, delivery orders.

2

Every field gets a confidence score

Shipper, consignee, container number, weight, value, port, dates. Not a blanket accuracy claim — a number on each field, on each document.

3

Values are checked against your own records

Your party list, your ports, container check digits, HS code format, totals that have to reconcile. A plausible-looking wrong answer gets caught here.

4

Anything uncertain stops and waits

It goes to a review screen built to be worked in seconds — document on one side, fields on the other, keyboard only. Your staff decide. The system never guesses.

5

Clean data lands in the system you already run

Your TMS by API where one exists, otherwise a structured file. Plus export-ready declaration data for your licensed people to review and lodge.

Where we stop

We do not file with the government. Ever.

Lodging a declaration is your licensed broker's function and it stays with them, along with the responsibility that comes with it. Lading Line prepares the data and hands it over. We give no customs advice and no tariff classification opinions.

Several vendors in this market are moving the other way, toward one-click filing. We think that quietly moves a liability onto software that cannot carry it.

Your documents never come to us

Flow runs inside an AWS account in Sydney that you own, pay for and control. You hold the root credentials, the encryption key and the billing. We publish the software; you run it.

We hold no credentials in your account at any point — not during set-up, not for support, not in an emergency. There is no key for us to lose and no back door for anyone to find.

What comes back to us is a count of documents processed for your invoice, and accuracy percentages for your monthly report. No shippers, no consignees, no values, no documents.

In practice
  • You create the AWS account on a call with us. About fifteen minutes.
  • You click one link. The system builds itself in about half an hour.
  • You log in and add your own integration credentials. We never see them.
  • Updates arrive as a change you approve. Nothing installs itself.
  • If you walk away, you delete it yourself and it is gone.
